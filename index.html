9 Nine Plus Two

Dodge & Survive!

How to Play

Dodge the buildings — don't let hit on it! Move your plane left and right to dodge every buildings.

Controls

  • Keyboard: ← → Arrow keys or A / D
  • Mouse / Touch: Click & drag on the canvas
  • Pause: Press P
  • Restart: Press R or Enter

Scoring & Difficulty

ScoreFalling ItemsSpeed
0 – 492 itemsIncreases every 10 pts
50 +3 itemsIncreases every 10 pts

Health

You have 3 health bars (top-right). Each hit removes one. When health hits zero — BOOM!

Last health bar? Your plane will blink as a warning!
